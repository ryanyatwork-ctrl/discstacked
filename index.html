DiscStacked – catalog your physical media

DiscStacked is a free app for cataloging and organizing a physical media collection. It covers movies (DVD, Blu-ray, 4K), music (CD, vinyl) and games on all platforms, plus a dedicated tab for concert videos and other music media.

What can you do with DiscStacked?

  • Catalog movies, music and games in one place.
  • Add items by barcode lookup or import an existing CLZ collection.
  • See what you own at a glance and stay organized as your collection grows.

Who is it for?

Collectors of physical movies, music and games who want a clean, accurate catalog of what they own. DiscStacked runs in the browser and installs as an app on phones and desktops.

Is DiscStacked free?

Yes. DiscStacked is free to use. Open it in any browser, or install it as an app on your phone or desktop.

DiscStacked is built by Belleville Systems.
